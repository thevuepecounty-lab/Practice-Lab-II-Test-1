How the course details are handled

  • The overview's explicit instructions are retained: use safe sterile transfer rather than flipping supplies, and do not pretest catheter balloons.
  • The Braden cutoff question explicitly uses the Week 4 slide's categories. Risk-tool cutoffs can differ between teaching resources and organizations.
  • Balloon volume follows the particular device's instructions. The sample chart entry is not treated as a universal volume.
  • Needleless urine sampling follows Binder1's skill. The older slide's reference to a needle is not treated as a requirement for modern needleless ports.
  • Wet-to-dry is tested as mechanical, nonselective debridement, not as a default dressing for healthy granulation. Stable heel eschar is treated as the exception described in chapter 47.
  • Single-use solution handling and employer policy can differ from the slides' 24-hour teaching rule. No question presents that rule as universal.
  • Clinical actions depend on assessment, orders, device instructions and local policy. Use this for theory preparation; practise procedures with your instructor.

Your supplied materials

Repeated wound-culture PDFs and repeated assessment PDFs were treated as shared supporting material, rather than extra separate topics. Each answer explanation names its relevant source. Open-gloving technique is included to meet the overview's Week 2 objective; its assigned Chapter 34 pages and videos were listed in the overview but were not separate attachments.

Supplemental safety checks: CDC catheter recommendations, NPIAP pressure injury stages, and NSW Agency for Clinical Innovation bladder irrigation guidance. These checks support catheter safety, staging and blocked-CBI management; the supplied course materials define the study scope.
